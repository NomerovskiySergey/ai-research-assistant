# Plagiarism Checker
Read AGENTS.md, docs/EVIDENCE.md, research/BRIEF.md and research/STATE.md. Verify hosted-processing permission and venue AI policy before handling confidential manuscripts.
Establish the target text and comparison corpus. Compare actually accessible passages; with no sources, describe the output as a limited wording/attribution review. Never imply database searches were run. Do not send confidential passages to web search without explicit permission.
Distinguish verbatim overlap, close paraphrase, common terminology, attributed quotations and reuse of the author's prior text. Overlap warrants human review; it does not itself establish misconduct. Do not claim AI authorship detection.
For each finding, give target locator, source E-ID and precise locator, overlap type, attribution context and recommended action. Record checkable findings as C-IDs linked to E-IDs in research/claims.csv. Separate source verification from claim support.
Suggest appropriate quotation, attribution or independently structured explanation preserving meaning. Do not rewrite to evade detection. Preserve numbers, uncertainty and evidence IDs.
Report the exact corpus and limits. Never invent uniqueness percentages or certify absence of plagiarism. Interpret supplied similarity-service reports with their actual corpus/settings limits.
Save requested reviews in research/ and revisions in manuscripts/. Update STATE.md with scope, inputs, findings, unresolved evidence, changed files, next action and base Git revision.

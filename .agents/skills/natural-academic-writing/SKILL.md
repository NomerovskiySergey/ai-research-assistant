# Natural Academic Writing

Read AGENTS.md and docs/EVIDENCE.md; start project work from research/BRIEF.md and research/STATE.md. Verify hosted-processing permission and venue AI policy for confidential manuscripts.

## Scope and routing
Use this skill for natural prose, paragraph flow and authorial voice in scientific articles, reviews and academic essays (реферати). Use scientific-writing for manuscript structure and evidence synthesis; english-translator-and-improver for translation; educational-content-creator for lessons and assessments. Apply this style pass within those workflows without repeating their full procedures.
Preserve the user's language, genre, venue requirements and terminology. If the user supplies a writing sample, learn its general register and preferences without copying distinctive passages. Without a sample, use restrained, clear academic prose. Do not impose a named author's voice.

## Writing decisions
Begin with the concrete problem or claim the paragraph develops. Explain the relationship between evidence and conclusion. Remove generic openings, inflated importance claims, empty transitions and repetition that adds no reasoning.
Prefer concrete subjects and precise verbs. Replace unnecessary nominalizations and bureaucratic phrases when meaning survives. Keep technical terms when they are necessary; vary syntax rather than swapping terms for imprecise synonyms.
Let paragraph length and sentence rhythm follow the argument. Combine related short sentences and split overloaded ones; do not mechanically enforce a sentence-length pattern. Use lists only for truly parallel material.
Make transitions convey the actual relation: cause, limitation, contrast, example or consequence. Avoid stock phrases and obligatory summaries.
Use analogies only when they clarify a mechanism and suit the genre; state where a simplification stops applying. Do not require humor, anecdotes, rhetorical questions, first-person narration or conversational asides.
Use first person only for documented author actions and when appropriate to the venue. Never invent personal experience, experiments, opinions attributed to the user, measurements or quotations to make prose sound human.
Retain required IMRaD sections, conclusions and reporting items. Natural voice must not erase methodological detail or uncertainty.

## Evidence-preserving revision
Keep numbers, units, time points, negation, causal strength, uncertainty, citations and C/E-IDs unchanged unless the user explicitly requests a supported substantive correction. Keep each citation attached to the claim it supports after restructuring.
For new substantive claims, follow the project C-ID/E-ID and precise-locator requirements; use [EVIDENCE NEEDED] where support is absent. Label hypotheses, proposals and invented teaching examples. A style pass is not factual verification.
Compare the revised passage to the input for omissions, added assertions and changed evidential strength. Provide clean text by default, with short notes only for consequential ambiguities or requested explanations.
Improve readability and the author's voice; do not promise AI-detector outcomes or claim human authorship. Preserve appropriate AI disclosure under actual venue rules.

Save requested drafts in manuscripts/ and evidence notes in research/. Before handoff update STATE.md with scope, inputs, findings, unresolved evidence, changed files, next action and base Git revision. Chat-only editing does not require new artifact files.
